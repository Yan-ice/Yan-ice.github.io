About

A short note on where I am and what I’m building toward.

I’m a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research centers on software systems security, especially operating-system security and embedded systems security, from kernel privilege separation to isolation on resource-constrained platforms.

Looking ahead, I am working on security for real-time operating systems (RTOS), aiming for isolation and least privilege that remain compatible with timing guarantees and deployable system software.
