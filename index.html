About

A short note on where I am and what I’m building toward.

I’m a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on systems security, especially kernel compartmentalization and privilege separation for commodity operating systems.

I care about designs that are portable across ISAs and OSes, strong enough to reason about, and concrete enough to ship—turning isolation mechanisms into systems people can actually deploy.
